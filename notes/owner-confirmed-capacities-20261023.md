# Apply owner-confirmed capacities without calling them predictions

The owner saved 23 genuine labels in the local calibration form: five chico,
five mediano, three litro, one pint and nine not_cup. The old all-or-nothing
classifier rejected every class and did not transfer these exact confirmations.

This change keeps owner review separate from model estimation. An image-bound
review index verifies the annotation hash, original manifest, event, crop, source
image, camera geometry and detector identity. Exact reviewed observations get the
owner label even when the predictive classifier abstains. not_cup retains its
original track and detector fields but is excluded from usable-cup/size summaries.
No sales, POS/manual closing totals, primary detector rejection flags or physical
delivery counts are created. Historical reviewed days can be updated independently.

The conservative selective classifier keeps the original maximum distance .28
and minimum margin .04. It requires >=3 correctly predicted held-out scene groups
and no confidently wrong predictions to activate a category. Ambiguous cases may
abstain instead of disabling an otherwise supported region. Genuine calibration:
mediano 4 correct / 5 held-out references, 1 abstained; noncup 4 correct / 9, 5
abstained. No confidently incorrect acceptance among these 23 calibration examples.
These are small-set internal checks, NOT an independent generalization guarantee.
Chico remains unvalidated; pint has one independent scene and litro only two.

Dry-run on copies of the real local ledger preserved all source IDs: Oct23 has
19 tracks, 14 local proofs, 8 owner exclusions, 6 usable references (2 chico,
4 mediano). Oct22 has 35 tracks, 9 local proofs, 1 owner exclusion, 8 usable
references (3 chico, 1 mediano, 1 pint, 3 litro). None were generated by the model.
Source payloads for both days passed the server's hash/revision/provenance checks.

Validation: 73 size/calibration tests, 112 collector/transport tests, 103 focused
server tests and TypeScript pass. Full web validation remains a deployment gate.
No browser opens, primary model/camera changes, migrations or credential changes.
